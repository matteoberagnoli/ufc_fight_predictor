How it works

Measure40 numbers known before the fight: age, a rating of who each fighter has beaten, strikes, takedowns, defence.
PredictGradient-boosted decision trees, eight versions averaged, turn the gaps between the two fighters into a chance.
ExplainSHAP splits each prediction into one push per input, so every pick comes with its reasons.
